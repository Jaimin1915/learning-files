## 2. Niche (stay inside this)
> **Timeless Hindu wisdom and philosophy, told as animated stories, for the problems of modern life.**

- **Sources:** Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Ramayana, Mahabharata, Chanakya Niti, Vedic philosophy, stories of saints and rishis
- **Problems:** overthinking, fear of failure, exams and career pressure, anger, comparison, discipline, heartbreak, self-doubt, money worries
- **Audience:** young people (18–28) in India and abroad, overthinking, looking for peace and clear answers
- **Every video:** one modern problem → one ancient story or verse → one tiny action to try today

## 3. Channel description (paste in YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info)
```
Ancient wisdom. Modern problems. One story at a time. 🙏

Overthinking at 2 AM? Afraid of failing? Stuck comparing your life to others?
Thousands of years ago, the rishis, Krishna and the great epics already answered these questions.

On Ancient Teachings, we bring those answers to life as animated stories from the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Ramayana, Mahabharata and more, and explain how to use them in your life today.

🎬 A new story every week
📖 Real verses, explained simply
✅ One small action you can try today

Subscribe and start your journey with the ancient teachings.
```
(~650 characters; YouTube allows 1,000.)

## 7. Settings
- **Country:** India
- **Upload defaults** (Settings → Upload defaults): Category **Education**, Language **English**, paste the "Subscribe" line into the default description
- **Audience:** "No, not made for kids" (set as default)
- **Altered/synthetic content:** answer **Yes** on every AI video

## 8. Channel page layout (Customization → Layout)
- **Channel trailer** (for people who haven't subscribed): the Gita 2.47 film
- **Featured video** (for subscribers): latest video
- **Sections:** Videos → Shorts → playlists (create later as you have 3+ videos each):
  - "Bhagavad Gita Lessons"
  - "Ramayana & Mahabharata Stories"
  - "Overthinking & Anxiety"
