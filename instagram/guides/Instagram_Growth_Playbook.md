## PART 1: How the algorithm works (Video A)

### 1.1 The algorithm is a matchmaker
Instagram has one goal: **keep people on the app as long as possible** (more time = more ads = more money).
So it tries to show each person the videos they will enjoy most. If it matches well, people stay. If not, they leave.

> **Your job is simple: help the algorithm match your Reel to the right people.**

### 1.2 What happens after you post (step by step)

1. **Digital fingerprint.** Instagram "watches" your Reel with computer vision, "listens" to the voice and audio, and reads the caption, hashtags, creator, and location.
2. **Topic mapping.** It combines all of that into one understanding: *what is this Reel about?*
3. **Fit score.** It predicts *who* will like this Reel most.
4. **Sample test: about 200 people.** It shows the Reel to the ~200 people with the best fit score. **Most of them are NOT your followers.** It wants to see if *strangers* like it.
5. **Result:**
   - 👍 **Positive data** → pushes to ~2,000 → ~20,000 → ~200,000 … until the data gets weaker.
   - 😐 **Neutral data** → re-calculates and tests on another ~200.
   - 👎 **Negative data** → stops almost immediately (this is "200-view jail").

Even viral videos slow down eventually: they simply run out of people who want to watch.

> 💡 **Followers matter less than you think.** The first test group is mostly strangers. So you can grow fast even with 0 followers, if strangers like your Reel.

### 1.3 The only two things you control

| # | Goal | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Help the algorithm find the RIGHT 200 people** | Same topic, same audience, every time (**audience matching**) |
| 2 | **Make those 200 people engage** | Better Reels: watch time, likes, saves, shares, comments |

### 1.4 Goal 1: Audience matching (stay narrow)
- Post about the **same topic** for the **same type of person** ("avatar") again and again.
- After a few similar Reels, the algorithm learns: *"This account = Topic X for Person Y."* Then it knows exactly whom to test with.
- If you post mixed topics (example: tech → health → politics), the algorithm builds a **blended** fit score, tests on the wrong people, gets weak data, and the Reel **flops**.
- ⚠️ **Even one viral Reel for the wrong audience can confuse the algorithm for your next several posts.** Sometimes you must say **no** to an idea that looks viral but fits the wrong audience.

### 1.5 Goal 2: The 3 metrics the algorithm judges

| Metric | Meaning | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|
| **Average watch time / % completion** | How many seconds people watch, and what % of the Reel | Instagram Insights → Reel → "Average watch time", "Skip rate" |
| **Engagement rate** | (likes + comments + shares) ÷ views | Calculate yourself (tracker in Part 4) |
| **Watch-time session share** | Of all the time someone spent on the app, how much was on *your* videos | Hidden: nobody can see it. It goes up when people watch several of your Reels in a row. |

### 1.6 The "Four Horsemen" of a high-engagement Reel
Your Reel needs all four:

1. **Relevant:** it solves a real problem your viewer has.
2. **Non-obvious and usable:** new information they haven't heard, that they can actually use.
3. **Easy to absorb:** they understand it on the first watch.
4. **Short distance to action:** they can do a small thing and get a real result.

(These same four also turn viewers into buyers later, if you ever sell something.)

### 1.7 Five ways to get more comments
1. **Take a hard stance.** People comment when they strongly agree or disagree. Sitting in the middle = few comments.
2. **Pick the contrarian side.** Say what most people think is wrong. People love to comment *"you're wrong."*
3. **Amplify the stance.** "This is a good way to handle stress" < "This one verse beats every self-help book ever written."
4. **Use things people already have strong opinions about:** famous brands, people, ideas, movements.
5. **Drive strong emotion.** The more people *feel*, the more they comment.

### 1.8 What does NOT matter (according to Video A)
> "Posting time does not matter. Hashtags don't matter. The captions themselves don't matter. The only thing that matters is making great videos for a specific avatar group across a narrow band of topics, over and over. That is the cake. Everything else is the icing."

**Note for you:** I earlier gave you 5 hashtags and a caption. Keep them: they cost nothing and help *humans* (the caption can ask people to save/share). But don't spend time on them. **Spend your time on the Reel itself.**

### 1.9 Research what already works
Study Reels that are already doing well in your niche. Look at: topic, hook, storytelling, why people got curious. (Video A promotes a paid tool, Sandcastles. You can do the same thing for free with Video B's "content pool" below.)

## PART 2: The 4-step system from zero (Video B)

### Step 1: Pick ONE niche
- One core topic only. Many topics confuse **viewers** visiting your profile, confuse the **algorithm**, and make creating content harder for **you**.
- Example: Creator 1 posts 3 travel Reels a week. Creator 2 posts 1 travel + 1 fitness + 1 fashion. Creator 1 wins travel, because they make *more* travel content.
- Choose what you are most passionate about, most experienced in, or most enjoy, and can stick to **for years**.

### Step 2: Build a "content pool"
1. Search your topic in the **Instagram search bar**.
2. Scroll the Reels. **Save** every Reel with **1 million+ views** into a saved folder called **"Content Pool"**.
3. Collect **at least 20**.

Two benefits: you get proven ideas, AND Instagram learns your interest and keeps showing you more viral Reels in this topic. That gives you unlimited ideas.

### Step 3: "Best of Nine"
1. Choose **3 Reels** from your Content Pool that you are able to recreate.
2. Recreate **each format 3 times** → **9 Reels** total.

### Step 4: Find the pattern
1. After 9 Reels, look **only at views** (for fast growth, ignore likes/saves/shares for now).
2. Take the **top 3** and ask: what do they have in common? Audio, caption, format, hook, on-screen title, visuals.
3. **Repeat that pattern.** Keep posting what works.

1. Instagram is a matchmaker. Help it match you.
2. Every Reel is first tested on ~200 mostly-strangers. Win them, and it keeps pushing.
3. **One niche** (ancient Hindu wisdom for modern problems) · **one avatar** (Rahul, 21, overthinking) · **one language**.
4. Every Reel: real problem → non-obvious verse → simple words → one tiny action.
