# 25 · Seedance Performance Guide (acting, voice, timing)
[Docs page](https://flick.art/docs/seedance-prompts)

How to direct **acting** with Seedance. No screenshots on this page; it's all about writing.

## Rule 1: Write what we can SEE or HEAR, not the emotion
| ❌ Emotion label | ✅ Visible / audible action |
|---|---|
| she is sad | her eyes stop moving for one beat |
| he looks suspicious | his jaw tightens slightly |
| emotional delivery | she takes one small breath before speaking |
| angry voice | low, breathy, restrained voice |

## Rule 2: One short shot = one acting beat
❌ He walks to the table, sits, and opens the book. (3 beats in 5s)
✅ He reaches the table and sits down. *(Open the book in the next shot.)*

## Rule 3: Say the order
Use: **first · then · after · only after · while**
```
She stays still.
After hearing his words, her eyes stop.
Her jaw tightens slightly.
She looks back at him but does not speak.
```

## Rule 4: Character and camera on separate lines
```
Character: Both walk forward at a steady pace.
Camera: The camera stays in front of them and moves backward at their speed. Same distance and eye-level framing throughout.
```
Give the camera **one** job.

## Rule 5: Time windows for longer shots
`0–3 seconds: …` · `3–6 seconds: …` · or "after a brief pause…"
They guide pacing. They're not exact.

## Rule 6: Break the voice into parts
| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| Volume | quiet, barely above a whisper |
| Pace | slow, with pauses |
| Pause | a brief pause before "leave" |
| Breath | one small inhale before speaking |
| Emphasis | slight stress on the last word |
| Delivery | restrained urgency |

**Full example:**
```
He says, "Who saw him leave?"
Low, direct and controlled. Natural pace.
A brief pause before "leave", with slight emphasis on it.
He does not raise his voice.
```

## Template
```
Character: [first action]. Then [next action]. Only after [trigger], [reaction].
Camera: [one camera instruction].
Voice: [Name] says, "[line]". [volume], [pace], [pause], [emphasis].
```

## Practice (paid plan, ~100 credits for 5s)
Krishna on the chariot, 5s:
*"Character: Krishna looks at Arjuna. After a brief pause, he smiles slightly. Then he says, 'Rise, Arjuna.' Camera: Locked medium shot. Voice: calm, low and warm. A brief pause before 'Arjuna'."*
Check: did the smile come **after** the pause, and the line **after** the smile?
