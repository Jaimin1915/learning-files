# 27 · Seedance Action Guide (fights)
[Docs page](https://flick.art/docs/seedance-action-guide) · Real example: [The Duel](https://flick.art/film/the-duel-1c01b2) (30s fight, one Seedance generation)

No screenshots on this page; it's all about writing.

## Rule 1: Every hit has 5 stages
| Stage | Write |
|---|---|
| 1. Preparation | weight shifts, foot plants, weapon draws back |
| 2. Attack | he closes the distance with one clear move |
| 3. Contact | where it lands / where the block meets it |
| 4. Reaction | the other recoils, slides back, loses balance |
| 5. Recovery | both regain footing, reset stance |

❌ "an intense, brutal, fast fight"
✅ "Arjuna steps forward and swings his bow at chest height. Karna blocks with his forearm. Karna slides back one step, then resets his stance."

## Rule 2: Give each reference a job
```
Arjuna's appearance matches @Image1 100%.
Karna's appearance matches @Image2 100%.
Use @Image3 for the location and lighting.
Use @Video1 only for choreography and timing.
```

## Rule 3: One short shot = one exchange
One attack + its reaction per shot. Long combos → split into several shots.
Use **first · then · only after** and time windows (`0–2s … 2–4s …`).
Add: **"real-time speed, no slow motion."**

## Rule 4: Keep the camera simple
Start with a **locked medium-wide shot** that shows both bodies and the weapon. Add camera moves only after the action works.

## Rule 5: More than one opponent
Say who attacks first, what the others do while waiting, and what triggers the next attack. **One attacker at a time.**

## Rule 6: Sound at the right moment
breath before the effort → cloth moving → **impact at contact** → breath out on the reaction → footsteps after.

## Template
```
[A] matches @Image1 100%. [B] matches @Image2 100%. Real-time speed, no slow motion.
0–2s: [A] plants his feet and draws his sword back.
2–4s: [A] steps forward and cuts diagonally at [B]'s shoulder.
4–5s: [B] blocks with his shield; metal clang.
5–7s: [B] is pushed back two steps, nearly losing balance.
7–8s: Both reset their stance, facing each other.
Camera: locked medium-wide shot, both bodies and the sword path always visible.
Sound: sharp breath, cloth swish, loud metal impact at contact, gravel under the feet.
```

## How "The Duel" did it
2 character turnarounds + 1 location image + a **4,400-word prompt** with **13 timed shots**, every hand and foot movement described. The secret was the **writing**, not the button.

## Practice (paid plan, ~105 credits with Seedance 2.5 Draft, 5s)
Use the template with **Arjuna Sheet** vs a new **Karna** sheet. Locked camera. **One exchange only.**
Check: can you see all 5 stages? Did the hit and the sound line up?
