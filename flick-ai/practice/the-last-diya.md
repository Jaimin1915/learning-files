# Practice Film: The Last Diya
3 shots · about 12 seconds · 16:9 · no dialogue · budget ~80 credits

**Story:** At dusk, a boy lights the last diya in a row. The wind keeps blowing out his match. He shields it with his hands, and the flame finally holds.
**Change:** from almost giving up → patient effort pays off.

## Character
**AARAV**, 8-year-old Indian boy, short messy black hair, brown skin, mustard-yellow kurta, white pajama, barefoot.

## Location
A flat village rooftop at dusk. A low brick wall with a row of small clay diyas on it, all lit except the last one. Purple-orange sky, other houses with lights in the distance.

## Script
```
EXT. VILLAGE ROOFTOP - DUSK

A row of clay diyas glows along a low brick wall.
Only the last one is dark.

Aarav kneels beside it and strikes a match.
A gust of wind. The flame dies.

He strikes another. He cups both hands around it
and brings it slowly to the wick.

The wick catches. The last diya glows.
Aarav sits back on his heels and smiles.

FADE OUT.
```
