TITLE: Do Your Work, Don't Worry About Results (Bhagavad Gita 2.47)
FORMAT: Animated short, 16:9, about 5-6 minutes
VISUAL STYLE: 2D cartoon animation, clean bold black outlines, flat colors with soft shading, warm cinematic lighting. Same style in every scene. Respectful, devotional depiction of Krishna and Arjuna.

=====================================================================
CHARACTERS (keep these EXACTLY the same in every scene)
=====================================================================

1. KRISHNA
- Dark blue skin
- Long curly black hair
- Golden crown with a red gem, peacock feather on the left side of the crown
- White U-shaped tilak with a red center line on the forehead
- Round gold earrings
- Yellow silk upper cloth (pitambar) over a red garment
- Gold armlets with a red gem
- Calm, gentle, wise expression; soft divine golden glow around him
- Role: Arjuna's charioteer and guide

2. ARJUNA
- Warm brown skin
- Long dark brown hair tied in a TOP BUN with a small gold ring (bun always stays)
- Golden armor with a sun emblem on the chest, golden shoulder guards and wrist guards
- Red sash over the armor, cream dhoti, golden shin guards
- Weapon: Gandiva bow (ornate golden-brown bow), quiver of white-feathered arrows
- Role: the great warrior who loses courage before the war

3. THE YOUNG MAN (modern day)
- Young Indian man, about 22 years old
- Messy black hair, brown skin
- Faded red t-shirt, grey pajama pants
- Role: represents the viewer; worried and overthinking

=====================================================================
LOCATIONS
=====================================================================
A. BEDROOM AT NIGHT: dark blue room, unmade bed, mountain poster and guitar poster on the wall, small note on wall saying "A BETTER TOMORROW", red digital clock showing 2:00 AM, window with stars.
B. KURUKSHETRA BATTLEFIELD: dusty plain at dawn, two huge ancient Indian armies, orange flags, elephants, chariots, golden sunlight and dust in the air.
C. THE CHARIOT: ornate golden chariot with carved patterns and red cushions, four white horses, a flag with Hanuman on top.
D. VILLAGE FIELD: green farm field at sunrise, simple Indian village in the distance.
E. STUDY ROOM AT SUNRISE: simple desk by a window, books, morning light.

=====================================================================
SCENES
=====================================================================

SCENE 1 - THE 2 AM WORRY (Location A)
VISUAL: The young man sits on the edge of his bed, staring at his glowing phone, worried. Clock shows 2:00 AM.
CAMERA: Medium shot, slow push-in toward his face.
MOTION: He scrolls with his thumb, blinks nervously, then sighs.
VOICEOVER: "It's 2 a.m. You can't sleep. Your exam result, your job interview, your business. What if it doesn't work out?"

SCENE 2 - THE PROMISE (Location A)
VISUAL: Close-up of the young man's tired face lit by the phone.
CAMERA: Close-up, static.
MOTION: He closes his eyes.
VOICEOVER: "Five thousand years ago, the greatest warrior of his time felt the same fear. And Krishna gave him an answer that can change your life too."
ON-SCREEN TEXT: "Bhagavad Gita - Chapter 2, Verse 47"

SCENE 3 - KURUKSHETRA (Location B)
VISUAL: Wide aerial view of two massive armies facing each other at dawn.
CAMERA: Extreme wide shot, slow pan from left to right.
MOTION: Flags wave, dust drifts, warriors raise conches.
VOICEOVER: "Kurukshetra. Two huge armies face each other. Conches sound. The war is about to begin."

SCENE 4 - THE CHARIOT (Location C)
VISUAL: The golden chariot with four white horses stands between the two armies. Krishna holds the reins in front; Arjuna stands behind him holding the Gandiva bow.
CAMERA: Wide shot, slow push-in.
MOTION: Hanuman flag waves in the wind, horses shift slightly.
VOICEOVER: "In the middle stands one chariot, pulled by white horses. The warrior is Arjuna. His charioteer is Krishna himself."

SCENE 5 - ARJUNA BREAKS DOWN (Location C)
VISUAL: Arjuna sits down in the chariot, both hands on his head, the Gandiva bow lying at his feet, quiver beside him. Army in the background.
CAMERA: Medium shot, slow push-in.
MOTION: His shoulders tremble. Hands stay on his head. Hair stays in the top bun.
VOICEOVER: "Arjuna looks at the other side and sees his own teachers, his grandfather and his cousins. His hands shake. His great bow, the Gandiva, slips from his hands. 'Krishna, I cannot do this.' He's afraid of the war, and of what will happen after it."

SCENE 6 - KRISHNA TURNS (Location C)
VISUAL: Krishna in the charioteer's seat, looking back over his shoulder toward Arjuna with a gentle smile. Divine golden glow behind him. Army blurred in the background.
CAMERA: Close-up over Krishna's shoulder, static with slight push-in.
MOTION: Krishna slowly turns his head and his smile grows warm and compassionate. Peacock feather sways gently.
VOICEOVER: "Krishna turns to him, calm, with a gentle smile. And he says:"

SCENE 7 - THE VERSE (Location C)
VISUAL: Krishna facing Arjuna, right hand raised in a teaching gesture, serene and compassionate expression, golden divine light.
CAMERA: Medium close-up, very slow push-in.
MOTION: Soft glow pulses, hair and feather sway. Krishna's mouth stays calm (verse shown as text).
VOICEOVER: "Karmanye vadhikaraste, ma phaleshu kadachana. Ma karma-phala-hetur bhur, ma te sango 'stv akarmani. You have a right to your work, but never to its results. Don't let results be your motive, and don't be attached to doing nothing."
ON-SCREEN TEXT: Sanskrit verse, then English meaning, line by line.

SCENE 8 - LESSON 1: YOUR EFFORT IS YOURS (Location D)
VISUAL: An Indian farmer in a simple dhoti and turban plants seeds in a green field at sunrise, focused and peaceful.
CAMERA: Medium shot, slow pan.
MOTION: He presses seeds into the soil and waters them.
VOICEOVER: "Lesson one: your effort is in your control. A farmer can prepare the soil, plant the seeds and water them every day. That part is fully his."

SCENE 9 - LESSON 2: RESULTS ARE NOT ONLY YOURS (Location D)
VISUAL: The same farmer stands in the field looking up at grey rain clouds gathering in the sky.
CAMERA: Low angle looking up past the farmer at the sky.
MOTION: Clouds move slowly across the sky.
VOICEOVER: "Lesson two: the result depends on many things. The farmer can't control the rain, the weather or the market. If he worries about the rain every night, it doesn't help his crops grow. It only takes away his peace."

SCENE 10 - LESSON 3: DON'T QUIT (Location C)
VISUAL: Arjuna stands tall in the chariot, lifting the Gandiva bow with determination. Krishna smiles beside him. Hair still in the top bun.
CAMERA: Low angle, slow push-in (heroic shot).
MOTION: Arjuna rises and raises the bow. Hanuman flag waves strongly.
VOICEOVER: "Lesson three: don't run away from action. Krishna isn't saying 'don't care.' He's telling Arjuna not to let fear of results stop him from doing his duty. And Arjuna stands up again and picks up his bow."

SCENE 11 - YOUR LIFE TODAY (Location E)
VISUAL: The same young man from Scene 1, now sitting calmly at a desk by the window at sunrise, studying with focus and a peaceful expression.
CAMERA: Medium shot, slow push-in.
MOTION: He turns a page, takes a calm breath, sunlight brightens.
VOICEOVER: "If you're a student, focus on today's study, not tomorrow's marks. If you're working, do today's task well and stop replaying 'what if.' If you're building something, keep building one step at a time. Do your best and let go of the worry."

SCENE 12 - BLESSING (Location C at sunset)
VISUAL: Krishna standing on the chariot, right hand raised in blessing, sunset sky, peacock feather glowing in the warm light.
CAMERA: Medium shot, slow pull-out.
MOTION: Gentle smile, soft divine glow, flag waves gently.
VOICEOVER: "Krishna's words still work five thousand years later: do your work, and leave the worry. If this helped you, subscribe for one lesson from the Gita every week. Jai Shri Krishna."
