## Narration (paste into Speaker 1 exactly as written)
```
Bad day? Watch this before you give up.
[dramatic pause]
You failed the exam. Someone left. Everything feels heavy.
[dramatic pause]
Five thousand years ago, Krishna told Arjuna something that still works today.
[dramatic pause]
[slowly] Maatraa-sparshaas tu kaunteya, sheetoshna sukha duhkha daah. Aagamaapaayino nityaah, taans titikshasva bhaarata.
[dramatic pause]
Heat and cold. Pleasure and pain. They come, and they go. None of it stays.
[dramatic pause]
Winter does not stay forever. Neither does summer. Your bad day is a season, not your whole life.
[dramatic pause]
So don't run. Don't break. Breathe, and let it pass.
[dramatic pause]
Tonight, write one line: this too shall pass.
[dramatic pause]
Follow for one ancient teaching every week.
```

## Meaning of the verse (for captions)
*Mātrā-sparśās tu kaunteya śītoṣṇa-sukha-duḥkha-dāḥ, āgamāpāyino 'nityās tāṁs titikṣasva bhārata.*
"O son of Kunti, the contact of the senses with their objects gives cold and heat, pleasure and pain. They come and go; they are not permanent. Endure them patiently, O Bharata."
